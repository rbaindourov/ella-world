    🎮 How to Play & Keyboard Shortcuts

    • Walk & Sprint: Press W A S D or Arrow Keys for your runway walk, and hold Shift to sprint!
    • Crystal Cavern Dungeon: Step into the glowing portal in East Plaza or press G (🌀 Dungeon) to farm Coins, mine Crystal Veins (E), open Wooden & Golden Vault Chests, and Jump-Stomp or Sparkle-Blast (E) Shadow Slimes & Crystal Bats!
    • Backpack & Live Friend Trading: Press I (🎒 Backpack) to drink potions, place Prism Blocks, or inspect rare relics, and click 🤝 Trade next to any online friend to swap items & coins!
    • Glam Salon Closet: Press C or click 👗 Glam Closet to mix & match Ella Forensics characters, hairstyles, satin/glitter gowns, and dungeon-exclusive Crowns & Prism Wings!
    • Dress to Impress Runway: Press F or click 💃 Runway to teleport backstage, strut down the illuminated catwalk, strike poses (1–8), and vote 1–5 ⭐ stars!
    • Jump & Sky Obby: Press Spacebar to jump, or R to warp to your Sky Obby checkpoint.
    • Look Around (3D Camera): Drag anywhere on the 3D view with your mouse and scroll to zoom in/out.
    • Voice Chat: Press M to Mute/Unmute your mic, or turn on Push-to-Talk and hold V to speak.
    • Co-Op 2-Story House & Interiors Builder: Press B in Starlight Plaza to build solid walls, stairs, hinged Doors & Windows (E or Click to open/close!), plus 3D Kitchens, Bathrooms, Bedrooms, and placed Backpack Loot Trophies! Press Q in Build Mode to rotate furniture 90°.
    • Legendary Starlight Ray Gun: Hunt monsters & Golden Vault Chests in Crystal Cavern for the Legendary 🔫 Starlight Plasma Ray Gun — equip it in Backpack (I) or Glam Closet (C) for 3-DMG ranged cavern zaps & Plaza laser light shows!
    • Kindness Rules & Debtor's Prison: Cursing or unkind chat deducts escalating Starlight Coin fines (even into negative debt!).
